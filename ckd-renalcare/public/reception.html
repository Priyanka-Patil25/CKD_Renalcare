    Check queue position and visit status

    Use the queue records returned by the backend. Do not infer completion from missing records.

  3. 03
    Record the visit

    Enter available patient information and dated measurements.

  5. 05
    Hand off for clinical review

    The doctor remains responsible for diagnosis and care decisions.
