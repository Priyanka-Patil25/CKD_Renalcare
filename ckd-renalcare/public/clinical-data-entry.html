Clinical assessment
Why this section matters

CKD assessment considers eGFR, urine albumin-to-creatinine ratio (uACR), and evidence of chronicity. A single abnormal result alone may not establish CKD.

◷
Chronicity evidence

Use previous dated results or clinician-confirmed history to assess whether abnormalities persist for at least 3 months. This form records visit data; it does not independently confirm CKD chronicity.
